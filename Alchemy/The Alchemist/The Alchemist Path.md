*Key Skill:* Alchemy
**Alchemical Study (Alchemist Key)**
*Activation:* #
You have studied the path of alchemy, gaining the following benefits:
* You gain access to Energy, beginning with a maximum Energy of 2 + your Awareness or Presence (whichever is higher).
* You can now use the basic alchemical actions.
* You gain the Alchemy skill on your character sheet, starting with 1 rank in it. 
Additionally, you gain the Adept goal "Design a Circle." After completing the goal, you gain its reward: You gain access to your own signature Circle, a corresponding Expertise, and may choose talents from the Circle Trees.

**Energized**
*Prerequisite:* Alchemical Study key talent
*Activation:* %
When you acquire this talent, your maximum Energy increases by a number equal to your tier. When your tier increases by 1, your maximum Energy does as well.

**Alchemical Crafting**
*Prerequisite:* Alchemical Study key talent
*Activation:* %
You can use alchemy to craft any form of item. Items worth [50 bx] can be crafted instantly via the Transmute action. Items worth any more use the normal crafting rules, but you may make an Alchemy test instead of a Crafting test. If you do, you gain @ on the test.

**Defensive Alchemy**
*Prerequisite:* Energized taleknt
*Activation:* #
When you use the Dodge or Avoid Danger actions, provided you have access to a Circle and can use it for transmutation, you may choose to spend 1 Energy in order to increase your Deflect by 1 until the beginning of your next turn.
Transmutation Circles (except for signature Circles) are spent as usual.

**Organic Alchemy**
*Prerequisite:* Alchemical Crafting talent or Defensive Alchemy talent
*Activation:* %
You can now transmute organic matter.
Transmuting unwilling living creatures always requires a transmutation attack against the target's physical defense.

**Living Weapon**
*Prerequisite:* Defensive Alchemy talent
*Activation:* %
Reduce the activation cost of your transmutation attacks by > (to a minimum of >).

**Medicinal Transmutation**
*Prerequisite:* Organic Alchemy talent
*Activation:* >
Spend 1 Energy to treat a conscious willing character within your reach, and roll the target’s recovery die as part of this action. Your target recovers health equal to the result of their recovery die + your ranks in Alchemy. 
This transmutation requires a Circle, and consumes it as normal (except for signature Circles).
