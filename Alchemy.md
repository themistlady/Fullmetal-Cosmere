## The Alchemy Skill
Unlike normal skills, you can’t access the Alchemy Skill until the Alchemist path (or another rule) grants you access. Otherwise, your Alchemy skill works much like other skills: 
* Alchemy uses your Intellect score. It is a cognitive skill. 
* Your Alchemy skill modifier is calculated by combining your ranks in it with the corresponding attribute score. 
* During character advancement, you can gain additional ranks in Alchemy in the same way you do for other skills.

## Basic Alchemical Actions

**Draw Circle**
*Activation:* >>>
You may draw a transmutation Circle onto a surface you can reach. Make an Alchemy test (see Transmutation in Practice for the DCs), drawing a functioning Circle on a success. On a failure, the Circle cannot be used for transmutation, but you are aware of the failure and may try again at any point.
In a non-stressful, low-pressure situation, you may choose not to roll the D20, instead treating the result as a 10. If, in addition to that you have ample time, such as by forgoing a Rest, you may instead treat the result as a 20.

**Transmute**
*Activation:* >
If you have access to a transmutation Circle within reach, you may use it to perform an appropriate transmutation.
You must be touching both the Circle and the object or surface that is to be transmuted, and spend the appropriate amount of Energy (see Transmutation in Practice for the Energy costs).
No test is required to Transmute.

**Sense the Earth**
*Activation:* >>
You align your Soul with the Tectonic Energy of the Earth, recovering Energy up to your maximum.

## Using Transmutation
### Comprehension and Circles
In order to perform a transmutation, you need to know the material that is about to be transmuted, its structure and properties. The transmutation Circle used, essential for transmutation, must be specific to the material to be transmuted.
Complicated substances or structures may require a corresponding Expertise to transmute.

### Deconstruction and Reconstruction
While having access to an appropriate Circle, a transmutation may be performed by channeling a flux of Energy to break down the physical structure of the material and then reform it. Without Energy, transmutation cannot happen.

### Equivalent Exchange
Alchemy is ruled by the Law of Equivalent Exchange. Its basic principle states that matter can neither be created or destroyed, meaning that a material that is transmuted must retain its same mass throughout the process. In addition, it states that an object or material made of a particular substance or element can only be transmuted into another object with the same basic makeup and properties of that initial material, again based on the notion that nothing can be created from nowhere.

### Alchemy Effects

#### Power Scaling
As you gain more ranks in the Alchemy skill, this not only increases your skill modifier for tests, but it empowers your alchemical effects.

| Ranks | Effect Die | Effect Size        |
| ----- | ---------- | ------------------ |
| 1     | d4         | Small (2.5 ft)     |
| 2     | d6         | Medium (5 ft)      |
| 3     | d8         | Large (10 ft)      |
| 4     | d10        | Huge (15 ft)       |
| 5     | d12        | Gargantuan (20 ft) |
#### Effect Die
When using Transmutation or related talents, you'll sometimes roll dice to determine effects like damage dealt or health recovered. When indicated, the size of these Effect Dice scales with your ranks in Alchemy, as shown on the Power Scaling table.

#### Effect Size
Transmutation can only affect objects or areas up to a certain size. This Effect Size increases with your ranks in Alchemy, as shown on the Power Scaling table.
Unless otherwise specified, you can't transmute an object if either its width, length or height exceeds you Effect Size.

#### Duration
Transmutations occur instantly and their effects are permanent. While a transmutation reaction may cause an immediate effect as a consequence of the reaction itself, such as a release of energy or a burst of force from displacement of matter, the transmutation itself is permanent and cannot be easily undone.

### Transmutation in Practice
Transmutation always requires access to a specific Circle, and you must be in contact with both it and the object or surface to be transmuted.
Transmutation always requires an expenditure of Energy, which may vary based on the specifics of the procedure.

#### Shaping Matter
When you transmute, you can immediately form a portion of matter into a rough shape as part of that action. This object or portion of the surface can’t exceed the Effect Size for your ranks in Alchemy (see the Power Scaling table). 
The Circle DC for shaping matter is usually 10. If the material being shaped is complex enough to require an Expertise to transmute, the DC is 15 instead.
The Energy cost for shaping matter is usually 1 per transmutation.
You can’t initially transmute organic matter with this ability.

#### Transforming Matter
When you transmute, you can rearrange the shape of an object of surface down to a molecular level. This object or portion of the surface can’t exceed the Effect Size for your ranks in Alchemy (see the Power Scaling table). 
The Energy cost for transforming matter is dependent on the number of ranks required to transform an object of that size ([1 for Small, etc.]).
You can’t initially transmute organic matter with this ability.

*Chemical Rearrangement.* You may isolate an individual element from a larger substance, or combine different elements into a single compound. You must know the exact composition of both your initial reagents and your expected product.
The Circle DC for chemical rearrangement is usually 15. If the material being shaped or expected product is complex enough to require an Expertise to transmute, the DC is 20 instead.

*Hydrokinesis.* Water is a simple and widespread compound, and can generally be worked for greater effect than other forms of matter transformation. Creating water from air or isolating it from liquids is considered hydrokinesis.
In addition, it is possible to transmute a source of water into changing states by one step (you cannot turn solid water into gas or vice versa) without massive expenditures of energy. Causing water to change states in this manner is considered hydrokinesis.
Hydrokinesis never requires an Expertise, and has a Circle DC of 10.

*Combustion.* Matter can be transformed to generate a combustion reaction and produce large amounts of energy. In order to perform a combustion transmutation, it is generally necessary to isolate/concoct some form of combustible matter, air alone is not enough.
The Energy cost for combustion does not depend on the amount of matter being used (though that is still limited by your ranks in Alchemy), instead being dependent on the number of ranks associated to the expected Effect Die, up to your maximum ([1 for 1d4, etc.]).
The Circle DC for combustion is usually 20.

### Sidebar: Crafting
In order to use transmutation to craft even the most basic equipment always requires an appropriate Expertise. Even then, only the more rudimentary items can be crafted initially, with intricate machinery requiring a talent.
